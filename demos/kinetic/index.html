01 Programs

Three lanes.
One standard.

Every program runs year-round in 10-week blocks. Join any week — the first session is free, and nobody gets put on the spot.

  1. 01

    Youth Wrestling

    Ages 8–18 · two age groups

    Builds balance, hand-fighting and the kind of grit that shows up in every other sport they play.

    Wrestling times in the weekly schedule
  2. 02

    Club Volleyball

    Ages 10–18 · 12U to 18U teams

    Builds first-contact passing, quick feet and the habit of talking on the court — teams that talk, win.

    Volleyball times in the weekly schedule
  3. 03

    Speed & Agility

    Ages 8–18 · all sports

    Builds how to land, brake, cut and accelerate safely — the mechanics behind getting faster at anything.

    Speed & agility times in the weekly schedule

03 Coaches

Coaches who’ve been there.

Fourteen coaches on staff. These three run the programs — and you’ll see them on the floor, not in an office.

  1. #01 — Wrestling

    Marcus Delaney

    Head Wrestling Coach

    Two-time state placer who drills the basics until they’re automatic — then makes them fast.

  2. #02 — Volleyball

    Tessa Okafor

    Club Volleyball Director

    Grew 14U from one team to five. Will happily talk your ear off about first-contact passing.

  3. #03 — Speed

    Jordan Reyes

    Speed & Agility Lead · CSCS

    Teaches kids to land, brake and change direction before he ever asks them to sprint.
