02 The detail

Three steps. The first two are the whole price. The third is optional, and it’s only offered once your site is live.

01

Step one

The demo

$19.99

One-time · build as many as you like

You answer a set of questions about your organization, and you get a draft of your own site back to look at.

  • Questions about what you do, who you serve, what you want the site to actually accomplish, and what’s not working now
  • A draft built from your answers — your programs, your seasons, your structure. Not a stock template with your name dropped in
  • It’s yours to keep looking at, for as long as you want
  • View only. You can look at it and click through it; you can’t edit it and neither can anyone else
  • Want to see a different direction? Build another for $19.99. Keep as many as you like

Straight about one thing

The self-serve demo builder and online payments aren’t switched on yet. Right now you email me and I do it with you by hand — same price, same result. I’d rather say that than show you a button that doesn’t work.

03

Step three · optional

Keeping it working

$49/mo

Cancel any time · no notice period

Optional. Offered after your site launches, never as a condition of building it. Cancel any time — no notice period, no phone call.

It covers monitoring and unlimited small changes. Medium and major changes are quoted first, and nothing starts until you say yes.

Turnaround times are targets, not guarantees. They start when a request becomes the active one.
ChangeExamplesOn the planTarget turnaround

Medium Change how a section looks, restyle, code changes, add a field to a form Quoted first — you approve before I start Quoted with the change
Major A new page, a new feature, removing or rebuilding a section, a redesign Quoted with the scope Quoted with the scope

Also on the plan · monitoring

I watch that:

  1. 01The site is actually up
  2. 02The domain is resolving
  3. 03Nothing has quietly broken

That’s the problem that started this whole business

Sorted before I start

I decide which category a request falls into before I start it, and I tell you if it’s bigger than you thought. I don’t reclassify something after the work is done.

Why medium and major are quoted

If $49 covered unlimited work of any size, I’d either go broke or quietly stop answering, and you’d be the one who found out. Quoting them means you always know the cost before anything happens — and it means I’m still here next year.

04 The limits

What I
don’t do.

Short list, and it’s honest rather than modest.

  1. 01

    Not offered: SEO as a service

    Not sold here

    I build a site that’s fast, structured and readable, which helps. I don’t sell ongoing SEO work and I won’t promise you a ranking.

  2. 02

    Not offered: Paid advertising

    No ad budgets

    I don’t run ads or manage ad budgets.

  3. 03

    Not offered: CRM & lead management

    Not my work

    CRM setups, email marketing or lead management. Not my work.

  4. 04

    Not offered: League software

    I build around it

    Registration, payments or league management software. If you already use a system for that, I’ll build around it and link to it properly.

“If you need those things, you should hire someone who actually does them.”

I’d rather tell you that than take the money.
