Start

Tell me about your club.

Easiest thing: email me at connect@webforgecreate.org. Or use the form below — it goes to the same place.

I'll look at your current site and send you what I'd change: how it loads, how it behaves on a phone, whether your domain is actually resolving, what a parent sees first. Free, and you're not obligated to anything.

Straight about one thing: the self-serve demo builder isn't switched on yet. Right now you email me and we do it together — same price, same result, just by hand while I finish the automatic version. I'd rather say that than have you click a button that doesn't work.

Send a note

I collect your name, organization and email only so I can reply to you. I don't sell it, share it, or add you to a mailing list. If you'd rather not use a form, just email me directly.

What happens next

  1. I reply, usually same day

    With what I found on your current site. Specific things, not a sales pitch.

  2. We talk for ten minutes if you want to

    You decide if it's worth going further. If I don't think I'm the right fit for what you need, I'll tell you that instead.

  3. If it's a fit, you get it in writing

    Scope, price, timeline. You read it before you pay anything.

No sales sequence. If you don't reply, I'm not going to chase you with five follow-ups. Reach out again whenever it makes sense.
