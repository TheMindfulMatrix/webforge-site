04 The $49 plan

What “small”
means, exactly.

This is the part most people are vague about, so here it is in plain words.

Three sizes of change · turnaround times are targets, not guarantees

  1. 01

    Small

    Unlimited · on the $49 plan

    Change text or a price, update hours or a schedule, swap a photo, move something, fix a typo, add a coach to the roster.

    Unlimited. Target: same or next business day.

  2. 02

    Medium

    Quoted first

    Change how a section looks, restyle something, add a field to a form, code changes.

    Quoted first, then done. You approve the price before I start.

  3. 03

    Major

    Quoted with the scope

    A whole new page, a new feature, rebuilding a section, a redesign.

    Quoted with the scope, before any of it starts.

I decide which category something is before I start it, and tell you if it’s bigger than you expected. I never reclassify something after the work is done.

Why I don’t include everything

If $49 covered unlimited work of any size, I’d either go broke or quietly stop answering — and you’d be the one who found out. I’d rather tell you now what’s included and actually do it.

05 Straight answers

Questions you’d be right to ask.

Five of them, with the short answer first and the whole answer next to it.

  1. 01

    Is the $19.99 refundable?

    If you don’t get a demo, yes

    It buys you a demo, and you get the demo. If you don’t get one, you get your money back. If you get one and just don’t like it, you’ve still got it to look at — and you’ve spent twenty dollars finding out, instead of signing a contract.

  2. 02

    What if I buy the demo and never build?

    You owe nothing

    Then you’ve spent $19.99 and you owe me nothing. There’s no follow-up sequence and no pressure. The demo is still yours to look at.

  3. 03

    Why $750 and not $5,000?

    One person, and early

    Because I’m one person and I’m early. This is what it costs right now, and I’d rather be honest about that than charge agency rates for a one-man operation.

  4. 04

    What about hosting?

    Worked out with you, directly

    I’d rather work that out with you directly — it depends on what you’re already paying and who you’re paying. I’m not going to put a number here that turns out to be wrong for your situation.

  5. 05

    What if I want to leave?

    You take everything

    You take everything. The site, the files, the content, the domain — yours from final payment, not held against a monthly fee. Cancel the plan and the site is still yours; it just stops being my job to change it.
