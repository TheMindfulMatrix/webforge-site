Turnaround times are targets, not guarantees.
ChangeExamplesOn the $49 plan
Small Change text or a price, update hours or a schedule, swap a photo, move something, fix a typo, add a coach to the roster Unlimited. Same or next business day

Why I don't include everything. If $49 covered unlimited work of any size, I'd either go broke or quietly stop answering — and you'd be the one who found out. I'd rather tell you now what's included and actually do it.

Straight answers

Questions you'd be right to ask.

Is the $19.99 refundable?

It buys you a demo, and you get the demo. If the system fails and you don't get one, you get your money back. If you get one and just don't like it, you've still got it on your shelf to look at — and you've spent twenty dollars finding out, instead of signing a contract.

What if I buy the demo and never build?

Then you've spent $19.99 and you owe me nothing. There's no follow-up sequence and no pressure. The demo stays on your shelf.

Why $750 and not $5,000?

Because I'm one person and I'm early. This is what it costs right now, and I'd rather be honest about that than charge agency rates for a one-man operation.

What about hosting?

I'd rather work that out with you directly — it depends on what you're already paying and who you're paying. I'm not going to put a number here that turns out to be wrong for your situation.

What if I want to leave?

You take everything. The site, the files, the content, the domain — yours from final payment, not held against a monthly fee. Cancel the plan and the site keeps working; it just stops being my job to change it.
