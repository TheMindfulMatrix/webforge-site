→ Why one club, four times

Same club. Four feels.

The club doesn’t change between the four demos. The feel does.

Same club, same three programs, same free-trial form — the style is what changes, along with the placeholder details. That’s the point: you pick how your club feels, and your information, your photos and your club’s colors go into it.

02 Things to try

Don’t just look.
Poke at it.

Each style has something it does that a screenshot can’t show. One thing to try in each.

  1. 01

    Kinetic

    Scroll fast

    The tapes and the running lanes behind the headline speed up and lean with your scroll. The footer swaps the whole site’s colorway.

  2. 02

    Cinematic

    Jump to a section

    Use the menu: the page cuts like a film edit instead of gliding. The end credits have a color-grade switch.

  3. 03

    Bright Club

    Tear the ticket

    Tap the free-trial pass and it tears off. Start the booking form and the pass fills in as you type.

  4. 04

    Precision

    Press G

    A layout grid drops over the page so you can see the system underneath. On a phone, it’s the Layout grid button in the footer.

Straight about the demos

Copperline Athletics is made up. The coaches, quotes, addresses and phone numbers are placeholders, and the forms in the demos don’t send anything. Want to see your own club in one of these styles? That’s the $19.99 demo. The self-serve builder isn’t switched on yet, so for now you email me and I make it with you by hand.
