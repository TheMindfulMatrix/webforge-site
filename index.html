What I actually found

Before building anything, I went through 35 real club and facility websites around St. Louis. 28 of them had something concretely wrong.

A domain pointing nowhere

One club with hundreds of athletes had every page return an error instead of the site. Every parent who searched them and clicked got nothing. Nobody there knew.

Pages too heavy for a phone

One site weighed 2.4 MB. A parent checking practice times on mobile data gives up before it finishes loading.

Seasons that ended two years ago

Stale schedules and dead footers. The site quietly tells a new family the program might not exist anymore.

None of these are design problems. They're maintenance problems. A broken domain isn't a site that was built badly — it's a site nobody was responsible for. That's the part I'm actually selling.
