01 The audit · July 2026

I checked
35 club sites.

Real clubs, gyms and studios around St. Louis. I looked at what a parent actually runs into: does the site load, does it work on a phone, is anything out of date. Here’s the scoreboard.

Four things I kept finding

  1. 01

    A domain pointing nowhere

    1 of 35 · fully offline

    A club with hundreds of athletes had every page return an error instead of their site. Every parent who searched and clicked got nothing. There was no way to tell how long it had been down.

  2. 02

    Pages too heavy for a phone

    Heaviest · 2.4 MB

    One page weighed 2.4 MB. A parent checking practice times on mobile data waits for every byte of it.

  3. 03

    Footers years out of date

    5 sites · as old as 2017

    Copyright years stuck at 2017, 2019, 2020. The site quietly tells a new family that the program might not exist anymore.

  4. 04

    Two live domains, same content

    One club, two addresses

    Two addresses showing the same site. Parents land on whichever one a search happens to show that day.

→ The pattern

Not a design problem. A maintenance problem.

“A broken domain isn’t a site that was built badly. It’s a site nobody was responsible for.”

That’s the part I’m actually selling. I build the site — and if you want, I keep watching it after launch.
